---
title: Take bus 407 to Sintanjin
order: 3
---

You can take Cheongju city bus **407** from Cheongju International Airport (청주국제공항) to Sintanjin Station (신탄진역) / Sintanjin-dong Administrative Welfare Center (신탄진동행정복지센터).

Bus 407 is a seat bus route listed by Cheongju Airport for **Sintanjin**. The route runs through Cheongju before reaching Sintanjin, so it is slower than the non-stop intercity bus to Yuseong, but it can be useful if you want to reach northern Daejeon directly.

The approximate service hours are from **05:40** to **22:35**, with buses about every **35 minutes**. The ride to Sintanjin usually takes about **85-100 minutes**, depending on traffic.

You can pay with a **T-money card** or another compatible Korean transit card. The adult fare is **KRW 1,650** by transit card. If you pay in cash, the adult fare is **KRW 1,750**.
