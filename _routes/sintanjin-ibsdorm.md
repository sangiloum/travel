title: Take a taxi
order: 100
---

From Sintanjin Station (신탄진역), it is recommended to take a taxi to the IBS Guesthouse/Dormitory. The ride is about 13 km and usually takes about **25-35 minutes**, depending on traffic.

Show this message to the taxi driver:

{: .important-title}
> Message to the taxi driver
>
> {: .text-alpha}
> **기초과학연구원 생활관으로 가주세요**
>
> Please go to the IBS Guesthouse/Dormitory.
